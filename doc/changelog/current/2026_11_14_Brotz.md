## [Main] - 2026/11/14

### Added

- MAJOR Add the polynomial-correction Navier-Stokes characteristic boundary conditions (PC-NSCBC) of Shehadi and van der Weide (2024) as non-reflecting inflow and outflow boundaries (`non_reflecting_inflow`, `non_reflecting_outflow`) to the explicit DG operator of the single- and multi-species compressible flow solver. For multiple species, the characteristic decomposition is extended by the species waves. The relaxation coefficients, the transverse relaxation coefficient of the outflow and the reference length are set in the subsection `compressible navier stokes/non reflecting boundary`.
